Delete your SpendLeak account

You can request permanent deletion from the website without reinstalling the Android app.

  1. Open SpendLeak in a browser and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings & privacy and choose Delete account.
  3. Confirm by typing DELETE. For a regular account, re-enter the current password.
  4. The app deletes the account and its associated sessions, preferences, imported transaction rows and import history from the active database.

This action cannot be undone. The hosting provider may retain disaster-recovery copies until their documented backup retention period expires.

Never send passwords, OTPs, or bank statements through public issues. General project issues can be filed on GitHub.
